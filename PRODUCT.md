# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

small business owners and sales teams who need simple, effective customer relationship management

## Product Purpose

Give small businesses enterprise-grade CRM without enterprise complexity

## Brand Personality

professional, organized, results-driven

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS templates with purple-to-blue gradients
- Over-designed dashboards where data is buried in decoration
- AI-generated interfaces that all share one look (Inter font, cream backgrounds, rounded everything)
- Enterprise apps that confuse density with professionalism

## Design Principles

- Pipeline visibility over data entry quantity
- Deals move forward, not sideways
- Contacts are context — link everything to a person
- Small business simplicity, not enterprise feature bloat

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance target
- Proper color contrast ratios (body text >= 4.5:1, large text >= 3:1)
- Keyboard navigable interface
- Screen reader friendly semantic HTML
- Support for prefers-reduced-motion and prefers-color-scheme
